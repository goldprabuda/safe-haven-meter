      How the meter is calculated
      • Each factor is ranked against its own trailing history (about two years for daily data, ten for CPI). 50 means typical; 90 means hotter than 90% of that history. Gold's own price is never an input.
      • Factor ranks average into four gauges. The meter blends them: fear bid 40%, rate & dollar headwind 30% (inverted, because a strong headwind pushes gold down), inflation impulse 15%, flows & positioning 15%.
      • Liquidity gate: when the junk-bond spread ranks above 90, the fear bid counts half. In a dash for cash, gold is sold with everything else (March 2020).
      • The trade read combines the meter with gold's trend against its 50- and 200-day averages. A high meter with a falling price is a divergence, not a buy signal.
      • Stale inputs count at half weight. Missing inputs drop out and the coverage figure under the dial falls.

      Sources: FRED (St. Louis Fed), Caldara & Iacoviello Geopolitical Risk Index (CC BY), CFTC Commitments of Traders, SPDR Gold Shares holdings, gold-api.com spot, COMEX futures closes. This product uses FRED data but is not endorsed or certified by the Federal Reserve Bank of St. Louis. Licensed inputs (ICE credit spread, GLD holdings) are shown as ranks only.
